# Fishies · press kit

Logos, screenshots and facts for anyone writing about [Fishies](https://fishies.lol).
Take what you need. Nothing here needs an email first.

The live version of this page, with every asset as a direct download, is at
**[fishies.lol/press](https://fishies.lol/press)** · that page is the one we keep current.

### Boilerplate

> Fishies is a browser game about keeping an aquarium. You raise fish from egg to adult,
> decorate a tank, and visit anyone else's tank at fishies.lol/@username. Fish keep growing,
> and get hungry, while you are away. Fishies is in open alpha at fishies.lol.
